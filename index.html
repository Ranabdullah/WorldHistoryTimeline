About World Myths, Writers & History

World Myths, Writers & History is an interactive vertical timeline running from top to bottom (10,000 BCE to 1800 CE).

Multi-Track Regional Dotted Lines: Each cultural region has its own vertical track with continuous dotted lines from top to bottom.

Google Photos Date Scrubber: Use the right-side rail to jump smoothly to any epoch in world history.

Box Zooming & Middle Mouse Pan: Adjust the box size slider to zoom cards in and out, and hold the middle mouse button or drag the background to pan anywhere.
